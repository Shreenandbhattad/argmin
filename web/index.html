How to film
  • Film for 1 to 2 minutes. A 15-second clip is not enough for a whole room.
  • Walk slowly along the walls facing inward, then make a second pass facing the walls.
  • Turn the lights on. Dim footage turns into blur.
  • Keep people out of the shot. Anything that moves leaves ghosts.
  • Keep edges or furniture in frame, not just a blank wall, the sky or water.
  • Save the video to the Files app and pick it from there. Picking from Photos can shrink it to 480p.
